# Code of conduct

This project is about old consoles and the people who keep them running. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews, is expected to keep it a place where anyone can ask a question or report a result.

## Expected

- Discuss the code, the hardware and the evidence, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone is wrong.
- Credit the work and the sources you build on.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or slurs of any kind.
- Personal attacks, or comments about someone's identity, background or experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Requests for, or links to, copyrighted BIOS images or game files.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through a [private advisory](../../security/advisories/new), the repository's private channel, and say it is a conduct report. Reports are read within seven days and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone whose behaviour breaks these rules.
